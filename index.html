Definitions of all variables used in the dashboard — derived from PSID microdata
Data: Panel Study of Income Dynamics (PSID), biennial waves 2005–2023. The PSID microdata have been cleaned and adjusted for misrepresentation and misreporting using: (i) adjustments to National Income and Product Accounts (NIPA) aggregates; (ii) reweighting to match the wealth distribution of the Survey of Consumer Finances (SCF); and (iii) reweighting to match the income distribution of IRS Statistics of Income records.
Reference: Gaillard, A., Wangner, P., Hellwig, C. & Werquin, N. (2023). "Consumption, wealth, and income inequality: A tale of tails." SSRN Working Paper 4636704.
Interface: This visualisation was built by Claude (Anthropic). At no point did the AI have access to household-level microdata — only the pre-computed aggregate statistics displayed here were provided as input. All data processing, cleaning, and estimation were performed entirely by the authors in R.
