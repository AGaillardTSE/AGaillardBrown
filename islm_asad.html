How to read
Top-left: Keynesian cross. Higher r lowers investment, shifts demand Z down, and reduces equilibrium output.

Bottom-left: IS-LM. Each point combines the goods-market equilibrium with the money-market equilibrium.

Bottom-right: Money market. Higher Y shifts money demand right and raises r for a given real money stock.

Demand shocks move the economy through the multiplier; supply shocks shift SRAS or LRAS; monetary policy moves LM directly.
